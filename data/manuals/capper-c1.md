## Capping torque
Rated torque 2.0–2.4 Nm. A slow upward drift of the torque indicates wear of the heads' magnetic clutch: schedule an inspection and recalibration within the shift. Immediate stop is not required while torque stays below 3.2 Nm.
Recommended first action: open a maintenance ticket and schedule a head inspection.

<!-- page: 3 -->
## Rejects
Capping rejects above 1.5% indicate crooked caps or bottles with irregular fill level coming from the filler: check upstream.
