# NT-4 outfeed conveyor – Maintenance Manual (SYNTHETIC, demo only)
Contents: 2 Gear motor · 3 Motor overheating · 4 Spare parts

<!-- page: 2 -->
## Gear motor
1.1 kW motor with cooling fan. Normal housing temperature 45–55 °C, alarm above 70 °C, stop above 80 °C.

<!-- page: 3 -->
## Motor overheating
A slow rise in motor temperature with nearly stable current indicates insufficient cooling (fan or grille clogged with cardboard dust) or overload due to speed.
Recommended immediate action, safe and reversible: reduce conveyor speed by 30–40% to lower the thermal load, without stopping the line. Schedule fan cleaning at the next planned stop (about 20 minutes).
No work on the energized motor is required.
