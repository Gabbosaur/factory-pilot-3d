# RF-24 rotary filler – Maintenance Manual (SYNTHETIC, demo only)
Contents: 2 Operating principle · 3 Fill-level rejects · 4 Filling valves · 5 Spare parts

<!-- page: 2 -->
## Operating principle
24 level-filling valves. Product comes from the header tank fed by the feed pump. Filling quality depends on stable inlet pressure and flow.

<!-- page: 3 -->
## Fill-level rejects
Fill-level rejects up to 0.7% are normal. Above 1.5% investigate; above 3% stop production.
If rejects rise on ALL valves at the same time and the inlet flow oscillates, the cause is almost always upstream (unstable supply from the feed pump), not in the filler: check the feed pump before working on the valves.
If rejects concern a single valve, check that valve's seal (p. 4).

<!-- page: 4 -->
## Filling valves
Seal replacement: 30–40 minutes per valve, machine stopped and made safe.
